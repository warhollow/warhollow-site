WARHOLLOW PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: August 2026

Warhollow LLC (“Warhollow,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides personal AI workspaces and related services that allow users to access and manage persistent computing environments, AI tools, models, files, and related resources (the “Services”).

This Privacy Policy explains how Warhollow collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit our website, create an account, purchase or use our Services, communicate with us, or otherwise interact with Warhollow.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including:

name and email address;
account and profile information;
subscription and product selections;
billing and transaction information;
support requests and communications; and
other information you choose to provide.

Account and Authentication Information

Warhollow uses Clerk to support account creation, authentication, session management, and account security, including multi-factor authentication.

Depending on how you authenticate, Clerk and Warhollow may process information such as your email address, authentication and session information, account-security status, and relevant device or browser information.

Payment Information

Warhollow uses Stripe to process subscription payments and provide certain billing functionality.

Payment card information is provided directly to Stripe rather than stored by Warhollow; we do not store such data. We receive information needed to administer your subscription, such as customer and subscription identifiers, your selected plan, transaction and payment status, and relevant billing events.

Stripe may process additional information for payment processing, fraud prevention, regulatory compliance, and related purposes.

Information Collected Automatically

When you use our website, portal, or Services, we may collect technical and operational information such as:

IP address;
browser and device information;
dates and times of access;
authentication and session events;
workspace identifiers;
environment status and lifecycle events;
application and system logs;
error and diagnostic information; and
security and abuse-prevention signals.

We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, maintain, and improve the Services.

2. Workspace Content

Warhollow provides computing environments in which you may store files, install software, use AI models and tools, and create or process other content (“Workspace Content”).

You control what you choose to place in your workspace.

Access to Workspace Content is restricted and may occur where reasonably necessary to provide support requested by you, maintain or secure the Services, investigate suspected abuse or security incidents, comply with applicable law, or protect Warhollow, our users, or others.

We use technical and organizational controls designed to limit privileged access to authorized purposes. We align with industry standards such as the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. 

Warhollow does not use Workspace Content to build advertising profiles or sell Workspace Content to data brokers.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right and authority to store and process content in your workspace.

3. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

create and administer your account;
authenticate users and protect accounts;
provide, provision, and maintain workspaces;
process subscriptions and payments;
provide customer support;
communicate with you about your account or the Services;
detect and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents;
maintain backup and disaster-recovery capabilities;
diagnose technical problems and improve service reliability;
enforce our Terms of Service and other policies;
comply with legal obligations; and
establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We may also use aggregated or de-identified information for analytics, capacity planning, security, and service improvement where that information does not reasonably identify an individual.

4. Service Providers

Warhollow uses third-party service providers to operate and support portions of the Services and our website. These currently include:

Clerk — identity management, authentication, session management, and account security.

Stripe — payment processing, subscriptions, billing, fraud prevention, and related payment services.

Cloudflare — DNS, secure network routing, access controls, security, and delivery of portions of the Warhollow website, portal, and workspace services.

Backblaze — encrypted off-site backup and disaster-recovery storage.

Formspree — website form submission and communications services. When you submit information through certain forms on the Warhollow website, such as a contact or inquiry form, Formspree may process the information you provide in order to transmit and manage the submission for Warhollow.

We may also use hosting, connectivity, hardware, software, monitoring, communications, security, and other technology providers as reasonably necessary to operate Warhollow.

These providers may process information on our behalf or for their own purposes as described in their applicable terms and privacy notices.

5. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose personal information:

to service providers that help operate Warhollow;
at your direction or with your consent;
where necessary to process transactions or provide requested functionality;
to investigate or prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats;
where required by applicable law or valid legal process;
where reasonably necessary to protect Warhollow, our users, or others; or
in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, sale of assets, or similar corporate transaction.

We may also disclose aggregated or de-identified information that does not reasonably identify you.

Warhollow does not sell personal information for money or use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Warhollow and our service providers may use cookies and similar technologies necessary to:

authenticate users;
maintain sessions;
remember account and security preferences;
prevent fraud and abuse; and
operate and understand the performance of the website and Services.

If we introduce non-essential analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies, we will provide additional notice or choices where required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain security and business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and its purpose.

Following cancellation or expiration of a workspace subscription, Warhollow will generally retain the associated workspace and Workspace Content for a limited period (typically 30 days) after the subscription ends to allow for recovery or reactivation. After that period, the workspace and associated Workspace Content may be permanently deleted.

Certain account, billing, transaction, security, and other business records may be retained for longer periods where reasonably necessary for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, or other legitimate purposes.

Deleted information may remain temporarily in encrypted backup systems until the applicable backup retention cycle expires.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we may delete or de-identify it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Security

Warhollow uses administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information and the Services.

Depending on the system involved, these safeguards may include encryption in transit, encrypted off-site backups, access controls, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, private administrative network access, monitoring, logging, and backup and recovery procedures.

No system can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and appropriately securing software, credentials, data, and other materials that you place within your workspace.

10. International Users and Data Transfers

Warhollow is based in the United States, and our Services are currently hosted and provided using infrastructure located in the United States. If you access or purchase Warhollow from another country, your personal information and Workspace Content may therefore be transferred to and processed in the United States.

By choosing to use the Services, you are requesting a U.S.-hosted computing environment and understand that providing the Services requires information to be processed in the United States.

Warhollow also uses service providers that may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required by applicable law, we and our service providers use appropriate safeguards or other legally recognized bases for international transfers.

You may contact privacy@warhollow.com if you have questions about the location or processing of your information.

11. Children’s Privacy

Warhollow’s accounts and subscription services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly permit children under 13 to create Warhollow accounts.

Customers may use their workspaces in connection with services or activities involving children, provided they do so in compliance with applicable law. Customers are responsible for determining whether laws governing children’s information apply to their use of the Services and for obtaining any required parental consent or other authorization.

If Warhollow learns that personal information has been provided directly to Warhollow by a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

12. Legal Requests

We may disclose information where we reasonably believe disclosure is required by applicable law or valid legal process.

Where legally permitted and appropriate, we may seek to limit requests that are overbroad or inconsistent with applicable law.
